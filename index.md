## Work

**[Simple](https://simple.org)**
Design lead · Almost 7 million patients · 5 countries
Simple is a free, open-source app that helps healthcare workers manage hypertension and diabetes care. I lead product design for the app, which supports nearly 7 million patients across India, Bangladesh, Ethiopia, Sri Lanka and Myanmar.

**[DHIS2 Android app](https://developers.dhis2.org/docs/mobile/mobile-ui/design-system)**
Design lead · 500,000+ healthcare staff · 60+ countries
I redesigned the core parts of the DHIS2 Android app, used in programmes such as hypertension, TB, malaria and HIV. I also designed the [hypertension and diabetes module](https://github.com/simpledotorg/dhis2-hypertension-package) that Nigeria uses to manage 60,000+ patients. With it, registering a new patient dropped from 12 to 4 minutes, and a follow-up visit from 4 minutes to 20 seconds.

**[HEARTS360 Dashboard](https://hearts360.org)**
Product and design lead · Indonesia · Philippines
HEARTS360 is a free, open-source dashboard for hypertension and diabetes programmes. I lead the pod building HEARTS360. It currently monitors 1.5 million patients in Indonesia and is being scaled up in India and the Philippines. Any electronic health record system that collects the required data can connect to HEARTS360 and display WHO indicators in under two days.

**[CallHub voter mobilisation](https://callhub.io/platform/ios-android-app/)**
Design lead
I led design for CallHub’s volunteer app on Android and iOS. Political campaigns and nonprofits use it to mobilise voters and supporters through calls and texts. Volunteers aged 15 to 80, often with little training, use the app. The challenge was to make it easy to pick up and use from the very first call.

## Working with AI

I use AI in my work and personal projects. At work, I’ve brought it into our product design and development workflows. We use BMAD to plan and build with AI agents, and PStack to coordinate multiple agents without getting in each other’s way.

Outside work, I enjoy experimenting and building things with AI. A few friends and I also started a monthly AI Saloon to share what we’re making and learn from each other.
