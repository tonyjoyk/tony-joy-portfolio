# Tony Joy

Product designer · Kerala, India

Hey, I'm Tony. I'm a product designer from Kerala, India.

I lead design at [Resolve to Save Lives](https://resolvetosavelives.org), a public health nonprofit where I design tools and services for healthcare staff.

Most of these tools run in busy clinics, on shared phones with slow internet, with about five minutes for each patient. I like to design for those limits.

---

## Work

**Simple** — [simple.org](https://simple.org)
Design lead · 5 million patients · 5 countries
Simple is an app that healthcare staff use to treat hypertension and diabetes. Clinics in India, Bangladesh, Ethiopia, Sri Lanka and Myanmar use it every day.
Current focus: statins to lower the risk of heart attack and stroke.

**DHIS2 Android app** — [dhis2.org](https://dhis2.org)
Design lead · 500,000+ healthcare staff · 60+ countries
DHIS2 is a health information system used in more than 60 countries. With RTSL and the University of Oslo, I redesigned patient registration in its Android app, from 12 screens down to 4.
Current focus: hypertension and diabetes care in Nigeria.

**HEARTS360 Dashboard** — [hearts360.org](https://hearts360.org)
Product and design lead · 1.5 million patients monitored in Indonesia
HEARTS360 is an open-source dashboard toolkit for heart health programmes, built on Grafana. Indonesia and the Philippines are now scaling it to national level.

**CallHub voter mobilisation** — [callhub.io](https://callhub.io)
Design lead
CallHub helps campaigns reach voters and volunteers. I led design for its mobilisation apps on Android and iOS. Our users were volunteers in the field, often with no training, little time, and a lot of noise around them.

## Side projects

**Walking for Nothing** — [Live](https://tonyjoyk.github.io/walking-for-nothing/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/tonyjoyk/walking-for-nothing)
A man walks through a field in Kerala. He never stops. His German Shepherd, Juhu, joins him now and then, and the weather changes. I drew all of it in code with p5.js, with no image files.

**Design Together** — Private repo
You write a brief. The tool pushes back, lists its assumptions, and asks questions. When the thinking is clear, it draws two lo-fi wireframes for the same screen. I made it to test if friction makes design with AI better.

**Medicine Tracker** — [Live](https://medicine-tracker-lyart.vercel.app/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/tonyjoyk/medicine-tracker)
I made it for my mom, to help her track her medicines.

**MemoryMoments** — Private repo
I tried to capture how a memory feels, and how that feeling changes with time.

## Ways I can help

- **Running design sprints** — In one week, we go from an unclear problem to something you can test with users.
- **User testing** — Five sessions, one week, and a clear list of what to fix.
- **Prototyping ideas** — A clickable prototype that you can show to real people, not a slide deck.
- **Aligning teams** — Product, design and engineering agree on what to build before they build it.
